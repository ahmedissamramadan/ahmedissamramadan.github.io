Privacy Policy

Application: AI Super User Pin Manager | Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

AI Super User Pin Manager is an internal, single-user administrative tool developed and operated solely by Ahmed Issam Ramadan ("AI Super User") to manage his own Pinterest business account via the official Pinterest API v5 and local MCP connectors.

2. Authentication & Data Access

  • OAuth 2.0 Authorization: Access is authorized strictly via official Pinterest OAuth flows.
  • Scope of Data: The application accesses only the authenticated owner's profile, Pins, boards, analytics, and advertising metrics where explicitly approved.
  • No Credentials Stored: The tool never requests, collects, or stores Pinterest passwords, session cookies, or payment information.
  • Local Storage: All OAuth tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) are held exclusively in local environment files (.env) on the owner's private local machine.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • No Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, distribute, or share any personal or Pinterest account data with third parties, external servers, or data brokers.
  • No Multi-Tenancy: The software does not provide public registration, external client logins, or multi-tenant hosting.

4. Revocation of Access

You can revoke API access at any moment directly from your Pinterest account settings under Connected apps or Security.
