# Privacy Policy for AI Super User Pin Manager

Last updated: October 2026

## 1. Overview
"AI Super User Pin Manager" is a strictly personal, single-user administrative tool developed and operated solely by Ahmed Issam Ramadan ("AI Super User") to manage his own Pinterest business account via the official Pinterest API v5.

## 2. Authentication & Data Access
- **Authentication**: Access is authorized strictly via official Pinterest OAuth flows.
- **Scope of Data**: The tool accesses only the authenticated owner's profile, Pins, boards, analytics, and advertising data where explicitly granted.
- **No Credentials Stored**: The application never requests, stores, or accesses Pinterest passwords, cookies, or payment information.
- **Local Storage**: All OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored exclusively in secure, private local environment variables (`.env`) on the developer's local machine.

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **Zero Third-Party Sharing**: We do not sell, rent, lease, monetize, distribute, or share any user or Pinterest account data with third parties, external servers, or data brokers.
- **Single-User Scope**: This application does not offer multi-tenant access or serve external end-users.

## 4. Revocation of Access
You may revoke the tool's access to your Pinterest account at any time directly through your Pinterest account settings under "Connected apps" or "Security".
